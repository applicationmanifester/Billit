# Privacy Policy for Billit

**Last Updated: March 15, 2026**

Billit ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

When you use Billit, we may collect certain personally identifiable information, including:

- **Identification Data**: Name, email address, and unique user identifiers provided by Google Authentication.
- **Business Data**: Business name, email, phone number, address, and Tax ID (GSTIN) that you provide for your seller profile.
- **Operational Data**: Client details (names, emails, addresses) and invoice details (items, prices, and totals) that you enter to manage your professional billing.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Data

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. However, the browser may store certain settings and data locally to improve performance.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

- Generate professional PDF invoices and receipts.
- Provide a dashboard of your financial stats (revenue, taxes, etc.).
- Enable cloud synchronization so you can access your data across devices.
- Facilitate the sharing and printing of your documents.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local Storage

Your data is stored locally in your browser's **LocalStorage**. This allows the app to function offline and ensures quick access to your records.

### 3.2 Cloud Synchronization

If you sign in with a Google account, your data is synchronized with **Google Firebase (Firestore)**. This data is stored securely in the cloud under your unique account ID, ensuring that only you have access to your business records.

### 3.3 Data Processing

All PDF generation and financial calculations are performed **client-side** in your browser. We do not process your business-sensitive data on our own servers.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Billit integrates with the following third-party services:

- **Google Firebase**: For authentication and data synchronization.
- **Browser Print & Share**: To enable printing and sharing functionality via your operating system.

Please refer to the privacy policies of these services for more information on how they handle your data.

## 5. Your Rights and Data Control

- **Access and Portability**: You can view all your stored data within the app interface.
- **Deletion**: You can use the "Wipe All Data" feature in settings to clear your local storage. If you are signed in, you can also delete your cloud records.
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: We do NOT sell or share your business data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the feedback section in the application settings.
